What is FOODIfAI?

FOODIfAI is an AI-powered cooking app that uses your camera to identify ingredients, suggest recipes, and analyze the nutritional content of dishes in seconds.

Objectives

Nutritional Guidance

Encourage users to explore nutritious and balanced meal options by providing detailed nutritional analysis.

Smart Ingredient Use

Help users utilize leftover or available ingredients effectively by suggesting suitable recipes.

Instant Suggestions

Deliver instant results for recipe suggestions, nutrient analysis, and meal visualization.
